The Small Modular Reactor, read through your lens

One design-basis package on the near-term SMR under deliberate military action — framed six ways, for the six audiences who read it differently.

The underlying material is the same: a facility-class Design Basis (DBA-MA-SMR-FC1) and the instruments around it. What changes is where you enter and what you read first. Pick the viewpoint that fits how you read, and walk an ordered path through the same package, framed for that role.

What this is

Nuclear power has always been designed against accidents. This body of work extends the design basis to deliberate military action — an intentional, adaptive adversary that can do what accidents cannot: deny site access, target the infrastructure shared across a multi-module campus, and defeat the very barriers a modern reduced-consequence licensing case depends on.

The layering is simple: a Design Basis (DB) sets what a facility must withstand; the Design Basis Accident (DBA) is the accident envelope; the Military-Action extension (DBA-MA) supplements — not replaces — both, and states, as a result rather than an open worry, where the SMR’s source-term and passive-safety arguments hold under deliberate action and where they do not.
